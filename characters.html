CHARACTERS

Main figures of the story

SNAKE

Main Character

He lives in the city.
He follows the rules.

He smiles when spoken to,
and keeps moving when not noticed.

The city was not built for him —
but he has learned how to exist within it.

Until one night
makes staying invisible no longer possible.

FOX

Main Character

He belongs to the city —
at least, on paper.

Born into a powerful family,
he understands how things work
long before anyone explains them.

He jokes.
He avoids questions.

Helping the snake is a small decision.

The consequences are not.
