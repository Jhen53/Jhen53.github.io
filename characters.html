CHARACTERS

Two lives, caught in the same city.

[ Snake Character Image ]

SNAKE

Main Character

Living quietly in the heart of the city, Snake has learned how to survive by staying unnoticed.

Lively, warm, and quietly hopeful, he longs to be seen — yet never dares to speak too loudly. His ordinary life is disrupted when his identity pulls him into a conspiracy far larger than himself.

Snake’s journey is not about becoming a hero, but about learning whether he is allowed to exist as who he truly is.

[ Fox Character Image ]

FOX

Main Character

Born into a powerful and respected family, Fox is charming, humorous, and impossible to read.

Trusting no one but himself, he keeps the world at a distance, treating rules and legacy as little more than jokes.

Helping Snake was never meant to be an act of kindness. But as the truth unfolds, Fox must decide whether he is escaping his family — or avoiding who he could become.
