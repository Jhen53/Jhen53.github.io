CHARACTERS

In this city, some residents are documented. Others exist only in the margins.

Snake

Main Character

A lively and optimistic snake who has learned how to survive without being seen. In a city that quietly erases his kind, he keeps smiling — even when silence is safer than speaking.

Fox

Main Character

Born into a powerful family, the fox treats trust as a liability. He helps the snake for reasons even he refuses to admit — perhaps to rebel, perhaps to survive.
