A MODERN CITY

A metropolitan city designed for order, efficiency, and harmony.

Different species live side by side, following the same systems, the same rules, and the same expectations.

Everything appears to work.

Some creatures are more visible than others.

Some differences are celebrated.
Others are quietly ignored.

The city does not call this discrimination.
It calls it structure.
