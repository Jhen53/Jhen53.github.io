FEATURED EVENT

Annual Grand Gala

The Annual Grand Gala is a formal event held in the Central District, bringing together invited guests from cultural, institutional, and private sectors.

The event is part of the city’s scheduled cultural program and is conducted under standard venue and security regulations.
